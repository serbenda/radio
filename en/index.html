What amateur radio is

🌍 Talk to the world

With an antenna on your balcony you can reach Japan, Argentina or a base in Antarctica. Shortwaves bounce off the ionosphere and travel right round the planet.

🛠️ Build and experiment

Home-made antennas, soldering kits, software-defined radios, satellites, weather balloons… It's an electronics lab with a licence to transmit.

🚨 Help out when everything else fails

When the networks go down in a flash flood (DANA), an earthquake or a blackout, radio amateurs keep communicating. That's why the hobby exists and is protected by law.

Your route

Your first month on the radio

Four weeks, from listening for the first time to being ready for the exam. Steps tick themselves off as you use the site, and each completed week earns you a badge. Everything is saved in your browser, with no sign-up.

Keep it in your pocket

Install it like an app

One tap and it's on your home screen, full screen and offline: the exam, Morse, lessons and simulated contacts all work on the Underground or out in the countryside. No app store, no sign-up and it takes up almost no space.

Choose your device and follow the steps.

🤖 Android
  1. Open bendi.dev/radio in Chrome (or Edge, Samsung Internet).
  2. Tap Install now above. If it doesn't appear, open the ⋮ menu → Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  3. Confirm with Install. The 📻 icon will appear with your apps.
📱 iPhone and iPad
  1. Open bendi.dev/radio in Safari (on iOS 16.4 or later, Chrome works too).
  2. Tap the Share button (at the bottom on iPhone, at the top on iPad).
  3. Scroll down and choose Add to Home Screen. Keep the name “radio” and tap Add.

Apple doesn't allow a direct install button, which is why you have to do it from Share.

💻 Computer
  1. In Chrome or Edge, click Install now or the install icon in the address bar.
  2. In Safari (macOS Sonoma or later): File menu → Add to Dock.
