Get ready for the exam

Amateur radio exam simulator

Just like the real exam: two parts, electricity and radio on one side and regulations and operating on the other, and you pass with half the answers right in each. Study with the flashcards first, which bring back what you get wrong; when the thermometer reaches 80 %, take the mock exam. The real exam is taken in Spanish; these are practice questions translated from the Spanish question bank.

Practice questions written from the official syllabus (Annex II of Order IET/1311/2013 (in Spanish)), not the actual exam questions. Your progress is saved only in this browser.
